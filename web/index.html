    A full Nasdaq day, 2.2× faster.

    Every message of the 30 December 2019 session, replayed per symbol through both books on an Apple M4 Pro. The two books are cross-checked against each other mid-session and must agree on every level. The speedup tracks book width, which is what the design predicts: indexing an array does not care how wide the book is, and a red-black tree does.
